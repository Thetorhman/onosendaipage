Automation · AI

Internal AI Platform & MSP Automation Tooling

  • Built a self-hosted internal AI portal in pure Python — Entra ID single sign-on (OIDC + PKCE), local PBKDF2 accounts, HMAC-signed sessions with idle and absolute timeouts, per-user revocation, and four automated test suites.

  • Automated recurring service-desk work — fleet-wide Acronis backup-health reporting, ConnectWise timesheet compliance reports and reminders, FortiGate license-expiry reporting with DKIM signing, and a Duo phone-provisioning tool with per-subaccount preview.
